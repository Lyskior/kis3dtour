Korea International School (KIS) was established in 2000, beginning with a campus in Seoul. In 2006, the school opened its main PK-12 Pangyo campus, which is located in Seongnam-si. This campus serves approximately 1200 students from pre-kindergarten through 12th grade. The high school, covering grades 9-12, enrolls over 400 of those students.

The mission of KIS is to inspire students with a passion for learning. It aims to cultivate the competence, self-assurance, initiative, and creativity necessary for success in the global community. The school's core values are Global Citizenship, Adaptability, Empowerment, Integrity, and Balance in Life.

KIS provides a PK-12 standards-based, American college preparatory education. The curriculum emphasizes applied learning, design thinking, and a strong STEM program. High school students must earn a minimum of 22 credits to graduate. The academic program is rigorous, offering over 25 Advanced Placement (AP) courses. KIS also offers the prestigious AP Capstone Diploma program, which includes AP Seminar and AP Research.

Core subjects include English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies. For English, courses range from thematic literature and composition to AP Language and AP Literature. The Mathematics department offers classes from Algebra and Geometry up to AP Calculus BC and Multivariable Calculus. Science offerings are extensive, including Biology, Chemistry, and Physics, as well as AP Environmental Science and AP Physics. Social Studies includes World History, US History, Economics, and AP Psychology.

KIS offers robust World Languages programs in Spanish, French, and Chinese, with levels from Novice to AP. A wide array of electives is available in Visual and Performing Arts, such as 2D Studio Art, 3D Sculpture, Theatre, Concert Choir, and Orchestra. The school also provides "Innovation" courses, including Computer-Aided Design (CAD), Media Production, Robotics, and Computer Science.

Student life is enriched by over 70 student-led clubs and activities. These include opportunities like Quiz Bowl, Forensics (Speech and Debate), HS Phoenix Theatre, and VEX Robotics. The KIS Phoenix athletics program fields teams across three seasons. Fall sports include Cross Country and Volleyball. Winter sports feature Basketball, Swimming, and Cheerleading. Spring sports offer Soccer, Badminton, and Track and Field. The school is committed to fostering a strong and respectful partnership between parents, teachers, and staff to create the best possible learning environment.
